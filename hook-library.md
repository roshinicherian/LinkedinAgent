## Formulas that suit this voice

Strong fit — practitioner credibility, no manufactured drama:

- **F17 Controlled A/B Anecdote** — one variable changed, two outcomes. Reads as
  evidence, not opinion. Best comment driver in the set for process posts.
- **F7 Odd-Precision Money Ledger** — number-first, the strongest 2026 opener.
  For Roshini use it as a **time** ledger, never a client-spend ledger.
- **F19 Anecdote-Meets-Evidence Bridge** — needs *three* sourced numbers. Only
  usable in a week where research lands three verifiable stats; otherwise pick
  something else rather than padding.
- **F15 Explain-to-Kids** — demystifying tracking/deliverability jargon. Saves.
- **F5 Self-Proving Meta** — a test run in public, results reported later.
- **F20 Diverging-Curves Close** — a closing move for F17/F7, not a whole post.

Use with care (2026 caveats):

- **F4 Time-Anchor Confession** — needs a specific dated uncomfortable fact and
  no announced candor. No "let me be honest".
- **F9 Curiosity-Gap** — must pay off within two lines, before the fold.
- **F12 Permission Slip / F18 False-Binary** — generic-frame devices; each
  survives only as the post's single contrast, with a dated fact behind it.

Avoid for this account:

- **F6 Comment-Gate Lead Magnet** — named target of LinkedIn's March 2026
  authenticity update. Not worth the risk on a reputation-first account.
- **F2 R.I.P. Obituary / F10 Contrarian + Historical Receipts** — both need
  6–9 dated receipt entries. Only if a research week genuinely produces them;
  never invent the timeline.
- **F14 Named Gratitude** — names colleagues. Ruled out by the brief.

## Hard hook rules (apply to every formula)

1. Never open with a question. −34% median likes. The question goes at the close (+3%).
2. Prefer a number-first line. +34% median likes.
3. Hook must survive the 210-character "see more" fold.
4. No all-caps line one. No emoji in the hook.
5. No "Here's what/how", no "Stop X, start Y", no "The result?" bridge.
6. One contrast and one triple per post, maximum.

*(Reach figures above are the linkedin-post-writer skill's own vendor/crowd data,
carried across as formula-selection heuristics. They are not cited in posts —
anything that appears in a post is verified independently and logged in
`stats-used.md`.)*
